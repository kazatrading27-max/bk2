About Mebek

A Dubai trading house built on reliability.

Mebek General Trading LLC is a general trading company based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. We source, distribute and export quality goods across the Gulf, Africa, Asia and Europe — moving everyday products from trusted manufacturers to the markets that need them.

We keep our operation deliberately lean: clear communication, dependable logistics and long-term supplier relationships. From a single pallet to full container loads, every order is handled with the same care — so our partners can plan with confidence.

Why Dubai

The gateway between East and West.

Dubai is not just where we are — it is how we compete. Few cities on earth can move goods between Asia, Africa and Europe with this speed and certainty.

01

Strategic gateway

Positioned at the crossroads of three continents, Dubai puts two-thirds of the world’s population within an eight-hour flight and the major shipping lanes at our quayside.

02

World-class ports & logistics

Jebel Ali is among the largest container ports in the Middle East, with free-zone warehousing, re-export corridors and customs clearance measured in hours, not days.

03

Business-friendly free trade

Low-duty regimes, free-zone structures and a stable, trade-first regulatory environment make the UAE one of the easiest places in the world to do business.
